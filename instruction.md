# LEAF LOVER — WEBSITE BUILD INSTRUCTIONS

## 01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a premium, modern, nature-focused website for **Leaf Lover**, a plant and gardening business.

Leaf Lover should feel like a modern nature brand rather than a traditional local nursery website.

The website must combine:

1. Brand awareness
2. Plant and product discovery
3. Online product ordering
4. WhatsApp-driven enquiries
5. Gardening and balcony setup services
6. Plant-care guidance
7. Real-time product availability
8. A manageable product catalogue
9. A foundation that can eventually scale into a large nature-focused company

### Core Brand Vision

> "Giant company for nature."

The website should communicate that Leaf Lover is building toward something significantly larger than simply selling plants.

---

# 02. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES

Prioritize the following goals in this order:

### Goal 1 — Make people want plants

The website should create an emotional connection with plants and nature.

### Goal 2 — Make discovering products effortless

Visitors should quickly understand:

* What Leaf Lover sells
* Which plants are suitable for them
* How much products cost
* Whether products are available
* How to order

### Goal 3 — Generate conversations

WhatsApp should be a major conversion channel.

Visitors should always have an easy way to:

* Ask about a plant
* Request gardening help
* Ask about availability
* Request balcony setup
* Contact Leaf Lover

### Goal 4 — Enable online purchasing

Products should support direct online ordering and online payment.

### Goal 5 — Sell services

The website should actively promote:

* Balcony setup
* Gardening services
* Plant-care support
* Plant Doctor consultations
* Gardening guidance
